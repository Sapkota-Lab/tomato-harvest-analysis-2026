3.0 – 4.5 Brix: Typical store-bought or mass-produced commercial fruit; often described as bland or watery.
5.0 – 5.5 Brix: Average fresh market tomatoes with standard flavor.
6.0 – 7.0 Brix: Good backyard garden tomatoes where proper brightness and true tomato flavor emerge.
8.0 – 9.5 Brix: Sweet cherry or specialty varieties (such as Sungold F1) grown in optimal conditions.
10.0 – 12.0 Brix: Exceptionally rich, dense, and sweet fruit, detailed further in The 10.0 Brix Tomato Challenge.
14.0+ Brix: Rare, contest-worthy sweetness found in ideal heirloom specimens.
